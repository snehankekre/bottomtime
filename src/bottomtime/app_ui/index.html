bottomtime

Your dives, from every computer, on one timeline

bottomtime reads the logs your dive computers already keep, lines up the ones that recorded the same dive, and keeps every channel they recorded, including GF99 and the deco ceiling. Everything stays on this Mac.

1

Shearwater

If Shearwater Cloud is installed and synced, bottomtime reads its dives directly.

2

Garmin Descent

Sign in to Garmin Connect once and bottomtime downloads your dives. Your password goes only to Garmin.

3

Anything else

Add .fit files from a Garmin, or a .db file from Shearwater Cloud's File → Export → Export Database.
